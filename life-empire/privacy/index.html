Privacy Policy — Life Empire

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Empire handles information when you use the game.

Information and services

Life Empire stores game progress, save slots, settings, gems, and purchased-entitlement status locally on the player's device.

The game does not currently require an account and does not intentionally transmit save-game contents or the player's chosen in-game name to the developer.

Advertising

Life Empire uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information needed to deliver, limit, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize or contextualize advertisements.

Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's policies and the consent choices available to the player.

Where required, Life Empire uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent before requesting ads. Players can review or change applicable choices through the privacy and cookie settings available in the game.

Withholding or withdrawing consent does not prevent the player from using the game, although ad availability may change.

In-app purchases

Life Empire offers in-app purchases through Google Play or the applicable platform store. These may include consumable or non-consumable digital items and a permanent Remove Ads purchase.

Purchases are processed by the platform store. The developer does not receive full payment-card details.

The store may provide transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, and related information required to grant purchased content, verify transactions, and restore eligible purchases.

Players who purchase the permanent Remove Ads product will no longer be shown advertisements covered by that purchase.

Data stored by the game

Game saves, profile information, settings, purchased entitlements, and related progress are stored locally on the device unless the operating system or store backup service copies application data as part of its own backup features.

Deleting the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored progress. Eligible permanent purchases may be restorable through the platform store where supported.

Children

Life Empire is not directed specifically at children. Advertising, privacy, and consent settings are configured according to the applicable Google Play target-audience requirements and platform policies.

Your choices

You may have privacy and advertising choices through your device settings, Google account, Google Play, and any privacy or consent controls available inside the game.

Third-party services

Life Empire may use third-party services such as Google Play and Google AdMob. These services have their own privacy policies and terms, and their handling of information is governed by those policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's services, features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the Life Empire Google Play listing.

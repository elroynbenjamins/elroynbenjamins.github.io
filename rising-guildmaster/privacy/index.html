Privacy Policy — Rising Guildmaster

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rising Guildmaster handles information when you use the game.

Information and services

Rising Guildmaster may use third-party platform and advertising services, including Google Play services and Google AdMob. These services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction information, diagnostics, approximate location derived from IP address, and similar technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Rising Guildmaster uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process information necessary to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize or contextualize advertising where permitted by law and by the user's consent choices.

In-app purchases

Rising Guildmaster offers in-app purchases through Google Play or the applicable platform store. These may include consumable or non-consumable digital items and a permanent Remove Ads purchase.

Purchases are processed by the platform store. The developer does not receive full payment-card details.

The store may provide transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, and related information required to grant purchased content, verify transactions, and restore eligible purchases.

Players who purchase the permanent Remove Ads product will no longer be shown advertisements covered by that purchase.

Data stored by the game

Game progress, settings, and purchased-entitlement status may be stored locally on your device unless a feature in the game clearly indicates that information is being stored or synchronized through an online service.

Children

The game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is processed by platform or advertising services under their applicable policies and consent requirements.

Your choices

You may have privacy and advertising choices through your device settings, Google account, Google Play, and any consent controls shown in the game.

Third-party services

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Their practices are governed by those policies rather than this policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its services, features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the Rising Guildmaster Google Play listing.

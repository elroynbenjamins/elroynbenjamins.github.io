Privacy Policy — Budora

Effective date / last updated: October 21, 2026

Budora is a free, ad-free, offline-first personal and household budgeting app for Android, developed by Elroy Benjamins. This policy explains what information is stored and when information may leave your device.

Privacy at a glance

Budora does not require registration or a bank connection. Your accounts, imported bank transactions, budgets, savings goals and household allocations are intended to remain on your device. The app does not include advertising, analytics tracking, or developer-hosted financial-data synchronization in its current release design.

1. Information you enter or import

You may enter account names and balances, transaction descriptions and amounts, spending categories, budgets, savings goals, loan details, planned payments, household members and local preferences. You may also select bank statement files to import. Budora processes these details locally to provide budgeting features and reports.

Budora does not ask for banking login credentials, connect directly to your bank or automatically retrieve statements. Importing a file is initiated by you using your device's file picker.

2. Local storage and safeguards

Budgeting information is stored in local app storage, including an on-device SQLite database and saved preferences. The financial database is not currently encrypted at rest by Budora. Budora offers an optional device-authentication screen lock, which restricts access through the app interface but is not database encryption. You should also protect your device with a strong screen lock.

Android's automatic cloud backup of Budora app data is disabled in the current app configuration. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, local records can be lost unless you made a separate backup.

3. Backups and exports

You can deliberately create a password-encrypted Budora backup or export transaction data. If you save or share a backup or CSV through a file manager, email, cloud storage or another application, that destination and its provider may process the file under their own terms and privacy policy. Unencrypted CSV exports require particular care. Keep your backup password secure; the developer cannot recover it.

4. Help, feedback and external links

When you choose Contact & Feedback, Budora prepares a draft for your email app addressed to developerelroy@gmail.com. The app does not automatically send it. If you choose to send the email, its contents and your email address are processed by your email provider and may be received and used by the developer to respond to you or resolve a problem. The app does not automatically attach your financial records. You can separately choose to include the app version and the country/bank names you select in the support form; these optional details are off by default. Budora does not read your stored bank accounts or transactions to fill in the support email. Please do not include account numbers, banking passwords or confidential statements in feedback.

Opening bank export guides, the privacy policy, or Google Play may open third-party websites or apps. Those services may process information such as IP address or interaction details according to their own policies. Merely using the offline budgeting features does not require opening these links.

5. Advertising, analytics and purchases

Budora's current offline-first release design does not include advertising SDKs, in-app purchases, subscriptions or developer-operated behavioral analytics. No paid bank-sync service or cloud account is required.

6. Children's information

Budora is intended for personal and household financial management and is not specifically designed for children. Household member labels are entered and managed locally by the person using the device; Budora does not automatically send those labels to the developer.

7. Your choices and data deletion

You can edit and delete supported local records from the app. You may also erase all app data through Android's application settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to your device's behavior. Doing so can permanently remove accounts, statements and preferences. Separately exported backups or files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted there if desired.

Because Budora currently has no online Budora account or developer-hosted financial records, there is no remote Budora financial account for the developer to delete. For deletion of information you intentionally sent in a support email, contact the address below with sufficient details to identify the correspondence.

8. Data retention and security

Local data remains until you modify or remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Messages you voluntarily send may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to answer requests, diagnose issues, prevent abuse or comply with applicable obligations. No technical security measure guarantees complete protection; treat exported financial records as sensitive.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when features, data handling or applicable requirements change. Material changes should be reflected in a new effective date. Always consult this page for the currently published policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and support requests: developerelroy@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy — VELDRYN

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VELDRYN handles information when you use the game.

Online services and account data

VELDRYN is an online game. Information required to provide multiplayer and online features may be transmitted to and stored on game servers or third-party hosting services.

This may include account or profile identifiers, character and progression data, inventory and equipment data, guild or multiplayer information, rankings, gameplay activity, technical diagnostics, device or session information, and other data needed to operate, secure, synchronize, and improve the online service.

If the game includes player-created names, chat, guild communication, or other social features, information submitted through those features may be transmitted to the online service and may be visible to other players as appropriate to the feature.

No advertising

VELDRYN does not display third-party advertisements and does not use Google AdMob for advertising.

In-app purchases

VELDRYN offers in-app purchases through Google Play or the applicable platform store. These may include consumable or non-consumable digital items, premium features, subscriptions, or other digital content offered by the game.

Purchases are processed by the platform store. The developer does not receive full payment-card details.

The store may provide transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, subscription status, and related information required to grant purchased content, verify transactions, and restore eligible purchases.

Online multiplayer and social features

VELDRYN may process gameplay and social information required for multiplayer features such as co-op play, guilds, rankings, market features, player profiles, and chat. Information that you choose to share through public or group features may be visible to other players.

Online activity may be logged or retained where reasonably necessary for service operation, moderation, abuse prevention, security, dispute handling, and enforcement of game rules.

Data storage and security

Online progression and account-related information may be stored remotely so that the game can provide persistent multiplayer services and synchronize progress. Some settings or cached information may also be stored locally on the device.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

VELDRYN is not directed specifically at children. Online and social features are intended to be operated in accordance with applicable Google Play target-audience, privacy, and safety requirements.

Your choices

You may have privacy choices through your device settings, Google account, Google Play, and controls provided inside the game. Where applicable, requests relating to account information or deletion can be made using the contact information or account-management options provided by the game.

Third-party services

VELDRYN may rely on third-party services for platform functionality, hosting, authentication, purchases, analytics, crash reporting, or other online functionality. These services have their own privacy policies and terms, and their handling of information is governed by those policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's services, multiplayer features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the VELDRYN Google Play listing.
